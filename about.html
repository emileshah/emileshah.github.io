✦ about me

nice to meet you.

I'm Emile — a student, researcher, and community builder at UC Berkeley.

who I am

I'm a student at UC Berkeley double-majoring in Data Science and Political Science. I'm drawn to problems that sit at the intersection of systems, people, and power — which is exactly where tech and policy collide.

Outside the classroom, I spend a lot of time in Berkeley's entrepreneurship community, working to connect and support women founders. I believe diverse founder communities don't just produce better companies — they change what problems get solved at scale.

what I care about

I'm passionate about closing the funding gap for women-led startups. The data is stark: despite strong performance metrics, women founders remain dramatically underfunded. I want to change that — through community, through research, and through amplifying the founders doing the work.

I'm also deeply interested in how data shapes political narratives. As a Data Science major, I've become increasingly aware of how the same numbers can tell very different stories depending on framing — and I write about that tension often.

how I work

I'm a connector at heart. I think the best outcomes happen when the right people are in the same room — or the same Slack channel. Whether I'm organizing events, doing research, or writing an essay, I'm always thinking about who else should be part of the conversation.

I write to think. Most of my articles start as a question I can't answer, and I write my way toward something more like understanding.

let's connect

I'm always happy to chat about startups, research, data, or Berkeley life. Find me on the platforms below, or just say hi — I genuinely love meeting new people.
